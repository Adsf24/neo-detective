# NEO DETECTIVE

Cooperative digital detective board game. 3–5 players on their phones investigate a pre-written case in a small but convincing digital world: mailboxes, computers, photos — and AI witnesses you can call and confront with evidence.

The truth lives in the engine, not in the prompt.
